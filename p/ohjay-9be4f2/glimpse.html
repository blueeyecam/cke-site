  • The home page does not show your phone number. It only appears once a visitor opens another page.
  • On the other pages the tap-to-call link is written wrongly (it starts "tel:-715"), so it may not dial on every phone. The email address cannot be tapped at all.
  • Sewer line jetting, sewer camera inspection, 3 year tank inspections, camper pumpouts and handwash stations are missing from the home page. They are only on the Services page.
  • No opening hours anywhere on the site.
  • The line Google shows under your name is only "Oh Jay Services LLC, Tomahawk". It does not say septic, pumping or portable toilets.
  • Your photo page still carries the site builder's sample text. Its hidden description talks about "programs and events" and its keywords are "Cafe, Bar, Drinks, Food, Schedule, Cocktails".
  • You have 37 good photos of your trucks and units. Only 5 have a caption, and none has a description a search engine can read.
  • Two spelling slips: "maintance" on the Services page and "I herby agree" on the Contact page.
  • There are no customer reviews and no common questions. The only way to ask for a quote is the Contact page form, which needs a spam code typed in.

We have already built an optimized front page that fixes these, using only your own words and photos. Your other pages stay as they are. The link is in our email.

  • Your Tomahawk Leader ad says "Fully Licensed and Insured". Your website never says it.
  • The trucks in your photos carry the words "Wisconsin Sanitary Licensee", a licence number (we read it as No. 2575) and a USDOT number. None of this is written anywhere on the site, so a search engine cannot see it.
  • Oneida County names you on its list of septic pumpers serving the county. Your site does not mention it.
  • You are an authorized Bio-Clean dealer, but only the Bio Clean page says so. The home page does not.
  • Your photo captions show pumper 002 added in July 2016 and pumper 003 in October 2016. A growing fleet is a good story, and it is buried in the gallery.

4. Google and reviews

  • Your website shows no customer reviews. It has a Facebook share button, but no link to a Facebook page or a Google listing of your own.
  • We could not open Google Maps for your business from where we work, so we give no star rating or review count here. Search your own name while signed out to see what a stranger sees.

  • A simple card or text asking each finished customer for a Google review would give the next customer something to read.
