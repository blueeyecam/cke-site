Common questions

Short answers, taken from what the current site already says.

How is a well drilled?

Most wells take three steps. We drill a hole down to bedrock. We install well casing into the bedrock. Then we drill through the bedrock until water of adequate flow is produced.

How deep will my well be?

There is no sure way to predetermine the actual depth of any well. Experience on similar wells in the area will generally provide us with a good estimate.

Where can the well go?

State law requires a 100-foot setback from your septic field and a 60-foot setback from the septic tank. Reduction of minimal setback may be applicable.

How much water do I need?

An average family will use up to 300 gallons per day. Each foot of hole stores approximately 1½ gallons, so 100 feet of water in the well is 150 gallons in storage. These guidelines are for a single family dwelling of four people.

Is my water hard?

More than 85% of all wells in Maine have hard water. We can provide you with a test and the right filtration equipment to correct your water problem.

Do you inspect wells for home buyers?

Yes. Our inspection covers water flow rate testing, water system equipment inspection, water quality testing, and bacteria and potability testing.
