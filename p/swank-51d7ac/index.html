What we do

From water well drilling to water treatment, we are a full service company that serves both residential and commercial customers.

Well drilling

We visit your site, give a free estimate, and work out the best location for the well and the pressure tank. The system is sized to the water you need for your family, commercial business or agricultural purposes.

  • Ingersoll Rand T3 drilling rig
  • Residential and commercial wells
  • Geothermal drilling

Pump installation and repair

We install high quality Goulds Pumps and we service other brands too. If your pump cannot be repaired, our trucks are fully stocked with replacement Goulds Pumps so you do not go long without water.

  • Electrical problems and bad motors repaired
  • Pump sizes from 1/2 horsepower to 5 horsepower, and larger if necessary
  • Constant pressure pumps that keep house pressure even

Water testing and treatment

We come out and test your water for hardness, iron, sulfur and pH levels. Once we have the results, we work out the size and type of filtration system you need and give a written, reasonable estimate.

  • Bacteria tests
  • FHA water tests
  • Lead tests
  • Nitrate tests

Well grouting and abandonment

To comply with township codes and safety standards, we fill in wells for customers. We have a special pressure grouter to achieve a complete and efficient fill.

  • Well abandonment
  • Well grouting

Four generations of well drilling

For more than 100 years, our 4th generation company has provided reliable service, quality craftsmanship and premium products. Most importantly, we care about you as our customer and strive for your complete satisfaction.

The current owner has more than 35 years of experience in the industry and a wealth of knowledge about everything related to water well drilling.

Common questions

Short answers, taken from what the current site already says.

Which areas do you cover?

Danville, Bloomsburg, Sunbury, Lewisburg, Elysburg, Catawissa, Benton and Williamsport.

My pump has quit. Do you come out after hours?

Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency service. Call (570) 275-1292.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Consultations and estimates are free. We visit your site and we come to you.

How long does a well pump last?

Generally, the average residential pump lasts about 10 years.

Which pumps do you fit?

We install Goulds Pumps and we service other brands. Our trucks carry replacement Goulds Pumps.

Can you test my water?

Yes. We test on site for hardness, iron, sulfur and pH. For bacteria, FHA, lead and nitrate tests we use a lab.

I have an old well that needs closing. Can you do that?

Yes. We fill in wells to comply with township codes and safety standards, using a special pressure grouter.
