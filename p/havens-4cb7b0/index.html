What we do

Residential, agricultural, commercial and exploratory wells, drilled with two T3W air rotary rigs.

Well drilling

Over a thousand home wells, dozens of farm wells, and commercial wells including the Troy borough wells, Bradford County Manor and Blossburg Municipality.
  • Hydrofracking low yield wells
  • Well cleaning
  • PVC well lining and telescoping
  • Downhole camera, extend casing

Pumps and tanks

We install and service Goulds Water Technology pumps and pressure tanks. As a Professional GPDA Member we can offer a 5 year warranty on them.
  • Submersible and jet pumps
  • Constant pressure systems
  • Booster pumps
  • Water lines, hand pumps

Water treatment

Free water analysis, then the right system, installed and serviced by us.
  • Water softeners
  • Iron and sulphur removal
  • pH neutralizing, sediment filtering
  • Chlorination, UV and ultra filtration
